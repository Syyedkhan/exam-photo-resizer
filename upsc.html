📖 UPSC Photo & Signature Size 2026 - Complete Guide

UPSC ne 2026 se photo aur signature ke rules update kar diye hain. Sabse bada change hai "Triple Signature" — ab ek hi image me 3 signatures upload karne honge.

UPSC Photo Requirements 2026:

  • Dimensions: 350×450 pixels
  • File size: 20 KB se 300 KB
  • Format: JPG/JPEG
  • Recent passport-style photo
  • White background

UPSC Triple Signature Requirements 2026:

  • Dimensions: 350×500 pixels
  • File size: 20 KB se 100 KB
  • Format: JPG/JPEG
  • Black ink me signature
  • Ek hi image me 3 signatures (running signature style)
  • White paper par

Triple Signature kaise banayein?

  1. Ek white paper par 3 baar signature kariye (teeno ek ke neeche ek)
  2. Us paper ki photo khinchiye
  3. Yahan upload kariye → "UPSC Triple Signature" preset chuniye
  4. Adjust karke Download dabaiye

Photo kaise resize karein?

  1. Upar "Select Photo" button dabaiye
  2. "UPSC Photo" preset chuniye
  3. Photo adjust karke Download dabaiye
