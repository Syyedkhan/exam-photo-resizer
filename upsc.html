UPSC Photo Requirements 2026 (Official Specifications)

UPSC (Union Public Service Commission) conducts India's most prestigious exams — Civil Services (IAS, IPS, IFS), NDA, CDS, and more. UPSC has strict photo and signature requirements, including a unique triple signature requirement. This tool helps you get everything right.

UPSC Photo Size & Dimensions

  • Photo dimensions: 350 x 450 pixels
  • Photo file size: 20 KB to 300 KB
  • Format: JPG or JPEG
  • Background: Plain white
  • Photo age: Recent (within 6 months)

UPSC Signature Requirements (Triple Signature)

  • Signature dimensions: 140 x 60 pixels each
  • Signature file size: 10 KB to 20 KB each
  • How many: 3 signatures (triple signature)
  • Ink color: Black ink on white paper

UPSC Exams Covered by This Tool

  • UPSC Civil Services (IAS, IPS, IFS)
  • UPSC NDA (National Defence Academy)
  • UPSC CDS (Combined Defence Services)
  • UPSC CMS (Combined Medical Services)
  • UPSC IES (Indian Engineering Services)
  • UPSC CAPF (Central Armed Police Forces)

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Only one signature instead of triple signature
  • Photo with dark or colored background
  • Photo with glasses or hat
  • Old photo (more than 6 months)
  • File size above 300 KB
  • Blurred photo
