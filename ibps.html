📖 IBPS Photo & Signature Size 2026 - Complete Guide

IBPS (Institute of Banking Personnel Selection) har saal PO, Clerk, SO jaise exams conduct karta hai. Form bharne ke dauran photo aur signature upload karna compulsory hota hai. Specifications strictly follow karni padti hain, warna application reject ho jata hai.

IBPS Photo Requirements 2026:

  • Dimensions: 200×230 pixels (approx 3.5cm × 4.5cm)
  • File size: 20 KB se 50 KB
  • Format: JPG/JPEG
  • Recent passport-style photo (6 mahine se purani nahi)
  • White ya light background
  • Chehra saaf dikhna chahiye
  • Photo me name aur date print nahi hona chahiye

IBPS Signature Requirements 2026:

  • Dimensions: 140×60 pixels (approx 3.5cm × 1.5cm)
  • File size: 10 KB se 20 KB
  • Format: JPG/JPEG
  • Black ink me signature
  • White paper par
  • Running handwriting me

IBPS Photo kaise resize karein? (3 Steps)

  1. Upar "Select Photo" button dabaiye aur apni photo chuniye
  2. "IBPS Photo" preset dropdown me chuniye
  3. Photo ko drag/zoom karke adjust kariye, phir Download dabaiye

IBPS Signature kaise resize karein?

  1. White paper par black pen se signature kariye
  2. Us paper ki saaf photo khinchiye
  3. Yahan upload kariye → "IBPS Signature" preset chuniye
  4. Adjust karke Download dabaiye

Common Mistakes jo avoid karein:

  • Photo me background white/light hona chahiye
  • Signature running hand me ho, capital letters me nahi
  • Photo blur ya low quality nahi honi chahiye
  • File size limit ke andar honi chahiye
  • Photo pe koi text ya watermark nahi hona chahiye

IBPS ke kaunse exams me ye specifications hain?

  • IBPS PO (Probationary Officer)
  • IBPS Clerk
  • IBPS SO (Specialist Officer)
  • IBPS RRB (Office Assistant, Officer Scale I/II/III)
