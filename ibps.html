IBPS Photo Requirements 2026 (Official Specifications)

IBPS (Institute of Banking Personnel Selection) conducts exams for PO, Clerk, SO, and RRB across India. Every applicant must upload a photo and signature. Each document has strict size and dimension requirements. If any document does not match, the application gets rejected. This tool helps you get both exactly right.

IBPS Photo Size & Dimensions

  • Photo dimensions: 200 x 230 pixels
  • Photo file size: 20 KB to 50 KB
  • Format: JPG or JPEG
  • Background: Plain white or light
  • Photo age: Recent (within 3 months)

IBPS Signature Requirements

  • Signature dimensions: 140 x 60 pixels
  • Signature file size: 10 KB to 20 KB
  • Ink color: Black ink on white paper
  • Important: Signature in CAPITAL LETTERS is NOT accepted

IBPS Exams Covered by This Tool

  • IBPS PO (Probationary Officer)
  • IBPS Clerk
  • IBPS SO (Specialist Officer)
  • IBPS RRB (Regional Rural Banks)

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Uploading a photo with dark or colored background
  • Signature in CAPITAL LETTERS — most common mistake
  • Photo with glasses or hat — not allowed
  • File size above 50 KB — rejected
  • Old photo (more than 3 months) — rejected
  • Blurred or low-quality photo

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the IBPS photo size in 2026?

IBPS photo should be 200x230 pixels with file size 20 KB to 50 KB in JPG/JPEG format.

Can I use CAPITAL LETTERS in my IBPS signature?

No. IBPS does not accept signatures in CAPITAL LETTERS. Use normal handwriting.

Is a white background mandatory for IBPS photo?

Yes, a plain white or light background is required. Dark or colored backgrounds are rejected.
