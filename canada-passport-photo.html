Canada Passport Photo Requirements 2026 (Official Specifications)

Canada passport photos must meet strict requirements set by Immigration, Refugees and Citizenship Canada (IRCC). Whether you're applying for a new Canadian passport, renewal, or visa, your photo must match these specifications exactly.

Canada Passport Photo Size & Dimensions

  • Photo size: 50 x 70 mm
  • Pixels: 591 x 827 pixels (minimum)
  • File size: Under 4 MB
  • Format: JPG or JPEG
  • Background: Plain white or light
  • Head size: 31 mm to 36 mm (chin to crown)

Canada Passport Photo Rules

  • Face the camera directly
  • Neutral expression — mouth closed
  • Both eyes open and visible
  • No glasses (unless medical reasons)
  • No hat or head covering (unless religious)
  • Even lighting — no shadows
  • Photo taken within last 6 months

Canada Visa Photo Requirements

Canada visa photos follow the same requirements: 50x70 mm, 591x827 pixels minimum, plain white background.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Smiling or showing teeth
  • Dark or colored background
  • Photo with shadows
  • Old photo (more than 6 months)
  • Blurred photo
