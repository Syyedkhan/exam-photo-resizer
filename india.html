Indian Government Exams Covered

SSC CGL Photo Size

SSC CGL requires photo 100x120 to 200x230 pixels (20-50 KB) and signature 140x60 pixels (10-20 KB).

IBPS Photo Size

IBPS PO, Clerk, SO, RRB require photo 200x230 pixels (20-50 KB), signature 140x60 pixels (10-20 KB), thumb 240x240 pixels (20-50 KB).

RRB NTPC Photo Size

RRB NTPC requires photo 320x240 pixels (15-40 KB) and signature 140x60 pixels (10-20 KB).

UPSC Photo Size

UPSC requires photo 350x450 pixels (20-300 KB) and triple signature 140x60 pixels each.

NEET Photo Size

NEET requires photo 200x230 pixels (10-200 KB) and signature 140x60 pixels (10-20 KB).
