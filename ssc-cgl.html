SSC CGL Photo Requirements 2026 (Official Specifications)

SSC CGL (Combined Graduate Level) is one of the most popular exams in India. Every year, lakhs of candidates apply. The Staff Selection Commission has strict photo and signature requirements. If your documents don't match, your application gets rejected. This tool helps you get them exactly right.

SSC CGL Photo Size & Dimensions

  • Photo dimensions: 100 x 120 pixels (minimum) to 200 x 230 pixels (maximum)
  • Photo file size: 20 KB to 50 KB
  • Format: JPG or JPEG
  • Background: Plain white or light
  • Photo age: Recent (within 3 months)

SSC CGL Signature Requirements

  • Signature dimensions: 140 x 60 pixels
  • Signature file size: 10 KB to 20 KB
  • Ink color: Black ink on white paper
  • Important: Signature in CAPITAL LETTERS is NOT accepted
