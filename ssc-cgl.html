📖 SSC CGL Photo & Signature Size 2026 - Complete Guide

SSC CGL 2026 ke online form me photo aur signature upload karna compulsory hai. Agar size sahi nahi hui, to form reject ho jata hai. Isliye exact specifications jaanna zaroori hai.

SSC CGL Photo Requirements:

  • Dimensions: 100×120 pixels
  • File size: 20 KB se 50 KB
  • Format: JPG/JPEG
  • Recent passport-style photo
  • White ya light background

SSC CGL Signature Requirements:

  • Dimensions: 140×60 pixels
  • File size: 10 KB se 20 KB
  • Format: JPG/JPEG
  • Black ink me signature
  • White paper par
