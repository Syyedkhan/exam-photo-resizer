NEET Photo Requirements 2026

NEET (National Eligibility cum Entrance Test) is conducted by NTA for medical admissions. Every applicant must upload a photo and signature with strict size requirements. This tool helps you get both exactly right.

NEET Photo Size & Dimensions

  • Photo dimensions: 200 x 230 pixels
  • Photo file size: 10 KB to 200 KB
  • Format: JPG or JPEG
  • Background: White

NEET Signature Requirements

  • Signature dimensions: 140 x 60 pixels
  • Signature file size: 10 KB to 20 KB
  • Important: Signature in CAPITAL LETTERS is NOT accepted
