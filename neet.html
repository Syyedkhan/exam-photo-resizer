NEET Photo Requirements 2026 (Official Specifications)

NEET (National Eligibility cum Entrance Test) is India's largest medical entrance exam. Conducted by NTA, lakhs of students apply every year. NEET has specific photo and signature requirements that must be followed strictly. This tool helps you meet them.

NEET Photo Size & Dimensions

  • Photo dimensions: 200 x 230 pixels
  • Photo file size: 10 KB to 200 KB
  • Format: JPG or JPEG
  • Background: Plain white
  • Photo age: Recent (within 6 months)
  • Important: Photo should include name and date (as per NTA)

NEET Signature Requirements

  • Signature dimensions: 140 x 60 pixels
  • Signature file size: 10 KB to 20 KB
  • Ink color: Black ink on white paper
  • Important: Signature in CAPITAL LETTERS is NOT accepted

NEET Exams Covered by This Tool

  • NEET UG (Undergraduate)
  • NEET PG (Postgraduate)
  • NEET SS (Super Speciality)

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Photo without name and date
  • Dark or colored background
  • Signature in CAPITAL LETTERS
  • Photo with glasses or hat
  • Blurred photo
  • Old photo (more than 6 months)
