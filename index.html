Indian Exam Photo Requirements 2026

Each Indian government exam has different photo requirements. Here's a quick guide:

SSC CGL Photo Size

SSC CGL requires a photo of 100x120 pixels to 200x230 pixels, with file size between 20 KB and 50 KB. Signature should be 140x60 pixels, 10 KB to 20 KB.

IBPS Photo Size

IBPS PO, Clerk, SO, and RRB require photo 200x230 pixels (20-50 KB) and signature 140x60 pixels (10-20 KB). Thumb impression 240x240 pixels (20-50 KB).

RRB NTPC Photo Size

RRB NTPC requires photo 320x240 pixels (15-40 KB) and signature 140x60 pixels (10-20 KB).

UPSC Photo Size

UPSC requires photo 350x450 pixels (20-300 KB) and triple signature 140x60 pixels each.

NEET Photo Size

NEET requires photo 200x230 pixels (10-200 KB) and signature 140x60 pixels (10-20 KB).

Passport & Visa Photo Requirements

US Passport Photo

US passport photos must be 2x2 inches (51x51 mm), 600x600 to 1200x1200 pixels, with plain white background.

UK Passport Photo

UK passport photos must be 35x45 mm (413x531 pixels minimum), with light grey or cream background.

Canada Passport Photo

Canada passport photos must be 50x70 mm (591x827 pixels minimum), with plain white background.

Australia Passport Photo

Australia passport photos must be 35x45 mm (413x531 pixels minimum), with white or light grey background.

Schengen Visa Photo

Schengen visa photos must be 35x45 mm (413x531 pixels minimum), with 70-80% face coverage and light background.
