UK Passport Photo Requirements 2026 (Official Specifications)

UK passport photos must meet strict rules set by HM Passport Office. Whether you're applying for a new British passport, renewal, or UK visa, your photo must meet these exact specifications. If it doesn't, your application will be delayed.

UK Passport Photo Size & Dimensions

  • Photo size: 35 x 45 mm
  • Pixels: 413 x 531 pixels (minimum)
  • File size: 50 KB to 10 MB
  • Format: JPG or JPEG
  • Background: Plain light grey or cream
  • Head size: 29 mm to 34 mm (chin to crown)

UK Passport Photo Rules

  • Face the camera directly
  • Neutral expression (no smile)
  • Both eyes open and visible
  • No glasses (unless medical reasons)
  • No hat or head covering (unless religious)
  • Even lighting — no shadows
  • Photo taken within last month

UK Visa Photo Requirements

UK visa photos follow the same requirements: 35x45 mm, 413x531 pixels minimum, light grey or cream background.
