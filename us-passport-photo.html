US Passport Photo Requirements 2026 (Official Specifications)

US passport photos must meet strict requirements set by the US Department of State. Whether you're applying for a new passport, renewal, or US visa, your photo must match these specifications exactly. If it doesn't, your application will be delayed or rejected.

US Passport Photo Size & Dimensions

  • Photo size: 2 x 2 inches (51 x 51 mm)
  • Pixels: 600 x 600 to 1200 x 1200 pixels
  • File size: Under 240 KB (for online)
  • Format: JPG or JPEG
  • Background: Plain white or off-white

US Passport Photo Rules

  • Face the camera directly (no tilt)
  • Neutral expression (no smile)
  • Both eyes open and visible
  • No glasses (since 2016)
  • No hat or head covering (unless religious)
  • Even lighting — no shadows on face
  • Photo taken within last 6 months

US Visa Photo Requirements

US visa photo requirements are the same as passport photos: 2x2 inches, 600x600 to 1200x1200 pixels, white background.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Smiling or showing teeth
  • Wearing glasses
  • Dark or colored background
  • Photo with shadows
  • Old photo (more than 6 months)
  • Blurred or low-quality photo

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the US passport photo size?

US passport photo must be 2x2 inches (51x51 mm), 600x600 to 1200x1200 pixels, with plain white background.

Can I wear glasses in my US passport photo?

No. Since November 2016, glasses are not allowed in US passport photos.

Can I smile in my US passport photo?

No. You must have a neutral expression with mouth closed.
