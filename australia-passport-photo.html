Australia Passport Photo Requirements 2026 (Official Specifications)

Australia passport photos must meet strict requirements set by the Australian Passport Office. Whether you're applying for a new Australian passport, renewal, or visa, your photo must match these specifications exactly.

Australia Passport Photo Size & Dimensions

  • Photo size: 35 x 45 mm
  • Pixels: 413 x 531 pixels (minimum)
  • File size: Under 500 KB
  • Format: JPG or JPEG
  • Background: Plain white or light grey
  • Head size: 32 mm to 36 mm

Australia Passport Photo Rules

  • Face the camera directly
  • Neutral expression — mouth closed
  • Both eyes open and visible
  • No glasses (unless medical reasons)
  • No hat or head covering (unless religious)
  • Even lighting — no shadows
  • Photo taken within last 6 months

Australia Visa Photo Requirements

Australia visa photos follow the same requirements: 35x45 mm, 413x531 pixels minimum, white or light grey background.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Smiling or showing teeth
  • Dark background
  • Photo with shadows
  • Old photo
  • Blurred photo
