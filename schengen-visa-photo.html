Schengen Visa Photo Requirements 2026 (Official Specifications)

Schengen visa photos must meet strict requirements set by the European Union. Whether you're applying for a visa to France, Germany, Italy, Spain, or any other Schengen country, your photo must match these specifications exactly.

Schengen Visa Photo Size & Dimensions

  • Photo size: 35 x 45 mm
  • Pixels: 413 x 531 pixels (minimum)
  • File size: Under 500 KB
  • Format: JPG or JPEG
  • Background: Plain light or white
  • Face coverage: 70-80% of photo

Schengen Visa Photo Rules

  • Face the camera directly
  • Neutral expression — mouth closed
  • Both eyes open and visible
  • No glasses (except medical reasons)
  • No hat or head covering (unless religious)
  • Even lighting — no shadows
  • Photo taken within last 6 months
