RRB NTPC Photo Requirements 2026 (Official Specifications)

RRB (Railway Recruitment Board) conducts exams for NTPC, Group D, ALP, and JE posts. Every applicant must upload a photo and signature that match the exact specifications. If your documents don't match, your application is rejected. This tool helps you get them right.

RRB NTPC Photo Size & Dimensions

  • Photo dimensions: 320 x 240 pixels
  • Photo file size: 15 KB to 40 KB
  • Format: JPG or JPEG
  • Background: Plain white or light
  • Photo age: Recent (within 3 months)

RRB NTPC Signature Requirements

  • Signature dimensions: 140 x 60 pixels
  • Signature file size: 10 KB to 20 KB
  • Ink color: Black ink on white paper
  • Important: Signature in CAPITAL LETTERS is NOT accepted

RRB Exams Covered by This Tool

  • RRB NTPC (Non-Technical Popular Categories)
  • RRB Group D
  • RRB ALP (Assistant Loco Pilot)
  • RRB JE (Junior Engineer)
  • RRB SSE (Senior Section Engineer)

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Photo with dark or colored background
  • Signature in CAPITAL LETTERS
  • Photo with glasses or hat
  • File size above 40 KB
  • Old photo (more than 3 months)
  • Blurred or low-quality photo
